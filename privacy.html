Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Moseler Management Company, LLC (“Moseler Co.,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, and protects personal information when you visit moselerco.com, buy our products or services, or use our software.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact information you give us, such as your name, company, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
  • Account information for our software, such as your login email and the settings and content you add.
  • Payment information. Payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. Card details go directly to Stripe and are never stored on our servers. We receive limited details from Stripe, such as the last four digits of your card, card brand, billing address, and payment status.
  • Usage information, such as pages visited, device and browser type, and IP address, collected automatically through standard server logs and essential cookies.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, operate, and support our products and services.
  • To process payments, send receipts, and manage subscriptions.
  • To respond to your questions and support requests.
  • To send service and billing notices. We only send marketing email with your consent, and every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, and to meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

3. How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers who help us operate, such as Stripe for payments and our hosting and email providers. They may use it only to provide services to us.
  • With companies we own and manage, when needed to provide a service you requested from them.
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights, customers, or the public.
  • In connection with a merger, sale, or reorganization of our business, subject to this policy.

4. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information. Our website is served over encrypted HTTPS connections, and payment data is handled by Stripe, which is certified to PCI-DSS Level 1. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you of a breach as required by law.

5. Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services, then only as long as needed for legal, tax, accounting, or dispute purposes.

6. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to stop marketing emails, by emailing moselerrealestate@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state law, and we will honor them.

7. Cookies

This website uses only the cookies needed for it to work. We do not use advertising cookies. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

8. Children

Our products and services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page with a new “Last updated” date, and will notify account holders of material changes by email.
