Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for products and services sold by Moseler Management Company, LLC (“Moseler Co.,” “we,” “us”). It applies to all purchases made through moselerco.com, our software, or an invoice we send you.

The short version: you can cancel a subscription at any time and you won’t be charged again. First-time subscription purchases can be refunded in full within 14 days. Duplicate or mistaken charges are always refunded.

1. Canceling a subscription

You can cancel any AI software subscription at any time, either from your account settings (where available) or by emailing moselerrealestate@gmail.com from the email address on your account. Cancellation stops all future renewal charges. Your access continues until the end of the billing period you already paid for, and then ends. We confirm every cancellation by email.

2. Refunds on subscriptions

  • First-time purchases: If you are not satisfied, request a refund within 14 days of your first subscription payment and we will refund that payment in full.
  • Renewals: Monthly and annual renewal payments are not refundable once the new billing period begins. You can cancel at any time to avoid the next renewal.
  • Annual plans: If you cancel an annual plan within 14 days of its renewal date, we will refund the renewal payment in full.
  • Billing errors: Duplicate charges, charges after a confirmed cancellation, and other billing mistakes are refunded in full, regardless of timing.

3. Management and advisory services

Services are governed by the written agreement or invoice for that engagement. Unless the agreement says otherwise, either party may end ongoing services with 30 days’ written notice. Fees for services already performed are not refundable. Any prepaid fees for services not yet performed are refunded on a pro-rata basis.

4. Returns and shipping

Our products are delivered digitally and our services are performed by our team. We do not sell or ship physical goods, so there is nothing to return.

5. How to request a refund

Email moselerrealestate@gmail.com with your name, the email on your account, the date and amount of the charge, and the last four digits of the card used. We respond within one business day. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method and usually appear within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

6. Disputes

If something looks wrong on your statement, please contact us first. We can usually resolve billing questions faster than a card dispute. Charges from us appear on your statement under the name Moseler Management Company or MOSELER CO.
